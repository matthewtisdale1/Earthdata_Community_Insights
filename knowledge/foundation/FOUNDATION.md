# Earthdata Community Insights

## Mission

Earthdata Community Insights (ECI) transforms community engagement into evidence-based investment priorities so Earthdata can focus development on capabilities that provide the greatest value to the greatest number of users.

## Why ECI Exists

Earthdata receives feedback through User Working Groups, workshops, surveys, Earth Action engagements, community meetings, GitHub issues, and future Science Sphere activities. This knowledge is distributed across reports, presentations, spreadsheets, and meeting notes, making it difficult to identify persistent needs, broad community impact, and the most important capability gaps.

ECI creates a traceable knowledge corpus that connects original evidence to Earthdata-wide canonical needs, capabilities, gaps, initiatives, and investment priorities.

## North Star

> If Earthdata invests its next dollar, where will it create the greatest value for the greatest number of users?

## Principles

- **Evidence first.** Recommendations must be traceable to original evidence.
- **Earthdata before organization.** Organizations contribute evidence; canonical needs belong to Earthdata users.
- **Human curated.** AI may assist, but people approve published knowledge and assessments.
- **Explainable decisions.** Scores and priorities must show the evidence and judgments behind them.
- **Investments before features.** ECI exists to improve development and investment decisions, not merely collect requests.
- **Historical continuity.** Evidence preserves the organization that originated it, while mappings may evolve as DAACs transition to Science Spheres.

## Success

ECI succeeds when reviewers and leadership can answer:

- What do Earthdata users need?
- How broadly and persistently is each need supported?
- What capabilities address it?
- How well is it addressed today?
- Which gaps offer the greatest potential benefit?
- What evidence supports the priority?

## Non-Goals

ECI is not a project-management system, issue tracker, document repository, or automated decision maker. Evidence counts must not be presented as user counts. ECI supplies transparent decision support; accountable people make investment and implementation decisions.

## Product Boundary

The knowledge corpus is the durable product. The API, database, and user interface are tools for curating, analyzing, and using that corpus.
